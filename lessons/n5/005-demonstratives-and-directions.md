## New grammar

### ここ・そこ・あそこ — place demonstratives

These refer to places:

- `ここ` — here, near the speaker
- `そこ` — there, near the listener
- `あそこ` — over there, away from both speaker and listener

Examples:

- `駅はここです。` — The station is here.
- `そこにレストランがあります。` — There is a restaurant there near you.
- `コンビニはあそこです。` — The convenience store is over there.

### これ・それ・あれ — standalone thing demonstratives

These stand in place of a noun:

- `これ` — this thing
- `それ` — that thing near the listener
- `あれ` — that thing over there

Examples:

- `これは何ですか。` — What is this?
- `それは私のチケットです。` — That is my ticket.

The key repair from Lesson 004 was that `そこ` denotes a **place**, while `それ` denotes a **thing**.

### この・その・あの + noun

These must directly modify a noun:

- `このチケット` — this ticket
- `そのホテル` — that hotel near you
- `あの駅` — that station over there

Examples:

- `このチケットは私のです。`
- `あの駅は京都駅です。`
- `あのレストランは静かです。`

### Relative locations — A の 上 / 下 / 前 / 左 / 右

Use `A の + location word` to locate something relative to A:

- `テーブルの上` — on/above the table
- `テーブルの下` — under the table
- `駅の前` — in front of the station
- `駅の左` — to the left of the station
- `駅の右` — to the right of the station

These combine naturally with the existence pattern:

**relative location + に + thing/person + が + あります / います**

Examples:

- `駅の右にコンビニがあります。`
- `ホテルの左にレストランがあります。`
- `ホテルの前に猫がいます。`

### な-adjective before a noun

A small but important extension arose during production:

- predicate: `その店は静かです。`
- before a noun: `静かな店`

The learner initially produced `静か店`, then correctly produced:

- `駅の右に静かなホテルがあります。`
- `あの静かなホテルは私のです。`

## New / activated kanji

| Kanji | Core meaning | Useful reading | Useful phrase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 上 | above / on | うえ | テーブルの上 |
| 下 | below / under | した | テーブルの下 |
| 前 | front | まえ | 駅の前 |
| 左 | left | ひだり | 駅の左 |
| 右 | right | みぎ | 駅の右 |

`上` and `下` were deferred placement items and were activated in this lesson. `前` was already known as vocabulary and was formally integrated as kanji. `左` and `右` were new active direction kanji.

## Useful language

- `それは何ですか。` — What is that?
- `このチケットは私のです。` — This ticket is mine.
- `あのレストランは静かです。` — That restaurant over there is quiet.
- `駅の右にコンビニがあります。` — There is a convenience store to the right of the station.
- `ホテルの左に友達が二人います。` — There are two friends to the left of the hotel.
- `このテーブルの下に水があります。` — There is water under this table.

Additional vocabulary encountered:

- `テーブル` — table; repeatedly misspelled as `ターブル`, so keep on short review.
- `チケット` — ticket.
- `建物（たてもの）` — building; encountered in comprehension but not independently retrieved.

## Comprehension

> A: すみません、駅はどこですか。  
> B: あそこです。ホテルの右にあります。  
> A: あの大きい建物ですか。  
> B: はい、そうです。駅の前にコンビニもあります。

The learner correctly understood:

- the station is to the right of the hotel;
- `あそこ` indicates a place away from both speakers;
- a convenience store is in front of the station;
- `あの` is used because it directly modifies the noun `建物`.

## Production

Successful mixed production included:

- `駅の右にコンビニがあります。`
- `ホテルの左にレストランがあります。`
- `ホテルの前に猫がいます。`
- `その店は静かです。`
- `駅の右に静かなホテルがあります。`
- `あの静かなホテルは私のです。`

Common errors repaired during production:

- using `あそこ` where `そこ` was required by listener proximity;
- using place demonstratives where a thing demonstrative was required;
- mixing `あそこに X があります` with `X はあそこです`;
- omitting `に` after a relative location;
- using `二つ` for people instead of `二人`;
- omitting `の下` when an explicitly relative location was required;
- using `います` for an inanimate shop;
- `ターブル` instead of `テーブル`.

## Session outcome

- **Demonstratives:** substantial progress. `そこ / それ` was repaired and `この / その / あの` became usable in production.
- **Directions:** strong. `上 / 下 / 前 / 左 / 右` and `A の + location` worked reliably after instruction.
- **Existence grammar:** much stronger than at the start of the lesson. `あります / います` and location `に` were repeatedly correct in direction sentences.
- **Action-location `で`:** opening error repaired; final shopping sentence used `店で` correctly.
- **People counters:** initial word-order instability repaired; `友達が二人います` was reliable by the final check.
- **Immediate weaknesses:** `九時（くじ）`, `二十日（はつか）`, `三つ` in mixed quantity production, and `テーブル` spelling.
