## New grammar

### Specific time + に

**Meaning:** に marks the specific time when an action happens.

**Formation:**

**[specific time] に + verb**

Examples:

- 三時に行きます。 — I will go at 3:00.
- 七時半に食べます。 — I will eat at 7:30.
- 明日、八時に駅に行きます。 — Tomorrow, I will go to the station at 8:00.

Relative time words such as 今日・明日・昨日 normally do not take に by themselves.

### 何時（なんじ） — asking what time

Use **何時に + action verb** when asking when an action happens:

- 何時に行きますか。 — What time will you go?
- 明日、何時に食べますか。 — What time will you eat tomorrow?

When the time itself is the predicate, use **X は何時ですか**:

- 電車は何時ですか。 — What time is the train?

Contrast:

- 何時に行きますか。 — asks the time of an action.
- 電車は何時ですか。 — asks the scheduled time of the train.

### ～から～まで — schedule ranges

**Meaning:** from ... to / until ...

**Formation:**

**[start] から [end] まで**

Examples:

- 三時から七時まで — from 3:00 to 7:00
- レストランは七時から十時までです。 — The restaurant is open from 7:00 to 10:00.
- 仕事は五時までです。 — Work is until 5:00.

Do not add に after a time immediately before から or まで.

## Kanji / time vocabulary

### Active focus

| Kanji | Core meaning | Useful reading | Useful word |
|---|---|---|---|
| 三 | three | さん | 三時（さんじ） |
| 七 | seven | しち / なな | 七時（しちじ） |
| 時 | hour / time | じ | 三時・七時・何時 |
| 八 | eight | はち | 八時（はちじ） |
| 半 | half | はん | 三時半・七時半 |
| 何 | what | なん / なに | 何時（なんじ） |

For **時**, clock-time compounds use **じ**. The reading **とき** is also common for 時 when it means "time/when" rather than an o'clock expression.

## Useful language

- 何時に行きますか。 — What time will you go?
- 電車は何時ですか。 — What time is the train?
- 七時半に行きます。 — I will go at 7:30.
- レストランは七時から十時までです。 — The restaurant is open from 7:00 to 10:00.
- 仕事（しごと） — work / job
- 店（みせ） — shop / store

The learner explicitly did not know the kanji for 仕事 and 店 during the lesson, so they remain new vocabulary rather than assumed kanji knowledge.

## Comprehension

> A: 明日、何時に駅に行きますか。  
> B: 七時半に行きます。  
> A: 電車は八時ですか。  
> B: はい、八時です。

Questions:

1. What time does B go to the station?
2. What time is the train?
3. Why does 七時半に行きます use に while 電車は八時です does not?

## Production

Use practical schedule language rather than isolated number drills:

1. Ask what time a train is.
2. Say when you will go to a station.
3. Give a restaurant or shop opening range with から / まで.
4. Mix time expressions with older location/existence grammar.

## Session outcome

- Final mastery check: **7/7**.
- The opening review exposed two short-term errors: 近くにコンビニがります omitted the あ of あります and final か, and 昨日、ホテルに食べました used に instead of action-location で. Both were repaired.
- ～ませんでした, 静かじゃないです, います, 前, あそこ, 友達 and 猫 were retrieved successfully.
- Specific-time に was initially omitted in 何時行きますか, but later became reliable in mixed production.
- The learner initially produced 何時に電車がですか for "What time is the train?"; the distinction between 電車は何時ですか and 何時に行きますか was subsequently retrieved correctly multiple times.
- ～から～まで was produced reliably. One shop-hours sentence omitted final です, but the schedule range itself was correct.
- 三・七・時 are now active learning items rather than deferred placement items.
- 五・九, 仕事, 店, ここ and そこ remain short-interval review targets.
