## New grammar

### ある / いる — saying that something exists or is somewhere

Japanese normally uses two different verbs for existence:

- **あります** for inanimate things: shops, stations, objects, buildings, events, etc.
- **います** for people and animals.

The important idea is not simply English "there is." These verbs also express where something or someone is located.

### Basic patterns

**Place に thing が あります。**  
There is a thing at/in the place.

- ここにコンビニがあります。 — There is a convenience store here.
- 駅の前にホテルがあります。 — There is a hotel in front of the station.

**Place に person/animal が います。**  
There is a person/animal at/in the place.

- ホテルに友達がいます。 — My friend is at the hotel.
- あそこに猫がいます。 — There is a cat over there.

### Why に is used

With ある / いる, **に** marks the place where the thing or person exists. This differs from the Lesson 001 use of **で**, which marks the place where an action happens.

Compare:

- コンビニ**に**水があります。 — There is water in the convenience store.
- コンビニ**で**水を買います。 — I buy water at the convenience store.

The first describes existence/location. The second describes an action performed there.

### Why が is used

In the basic existence pattern, **が** marks the thing or person whose existence is being introduced.

- コンビニがあります。 — There is a convenience store.
- 友達がいます。 — A friend is there / I have a friend there, depending on context.

Do not reduce が to a single English translation. For this lesson, focus on recognising its role in the existence pattern.

### Questions

- 近くにコンビニがありますか。 — Is there a convenience store nearby?
- 駅にタクシーがありますか。 — Are there taxis at the station?
- 田中さんはいますか。 — Is Tanaka here?

Teach the structure fully before uncued production. Start with guided contrasts between あります and います, then remove cues.

## Location words

Teach these through practical questions and answers:

- ここ — here
- そこ — there, near the listener
- あそこ — over there, away from both speaker and listener
- どこ — where
- 近く（ちかく） — nearby
- 前（まえ） — in front

Do not require new kanji recognition for 近 / 前 yet; reading support should remain visible while the grammar is new.

## Useful language

- 近くにコンビニがありますか。 — Is there a convenience store nearby?
- 駅はどこですか。 — Where is the station?
- あそこにあります。 — It is over there.
- ホテルに友達がいます。 — My friend is at the hotel.
- 駅の前です。 — It is in front of the station.

## Comprehension

Present without translation first:

> A: すみません、近くにコンビニがありますか。  
> B: はい、あります。駅の前です。  
> A: あそこですか。  
> B: はい、そうです。

Ask:

1. What is A looking for?
2. Does it exist nearby?
3. Where is it?

Only explain unknown vocabulary after the learner has attempted comprehension.

## Production

After guided practice, ask the learner to say naturally:

1. "Is there a convenience store nearby?"
2. "There is a hotel in front of the station."
3. "My friend is at the hotel."
4. Contrast two sentences using the same place: one with に + あります/います and one with で + an action verb.

## Mastery check

Use uncued or lightly cued retrieval:

1. Choose あります or います for an object and a person.
2. Fill the particles: 駅の前___コンビニ___あります。
3. Translate: "There is a cat over there."
4. Translate: "I bought water at the convenience store yesterday." to ensure Lesson 001 remains available.
5. Read and explain two items from 電車 / 明日 / 昨日 / 今日 / 買います / 行きます / 食べます.

- 駅の前 was initially understood as "at the station" rather than "in front of the station," then used correctly in subsequent production.
- Final uncued mastery check: **5/5**.
- No new kanji batch was introduced. 三・七・時 remain deferred for the next lesson.
