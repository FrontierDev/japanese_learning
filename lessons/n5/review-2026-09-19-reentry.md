# Review — Re-entry After Study Gap

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Level:** N5  
**Phase:** 1 — Navigation and basic interactions

## Purpose

This was a review/re-entry session after an 18-day gap since Lesson 005. No new grammar, kanji, or planned Lesson 006 material was introduced.

## Review targets

- Immediate weak readings: 九時（くじ）, 二十日（はつか）, 何人（なんにん）
- Counters: ～つ versus ～人
- Past/non-past selection with 昨日 / 明日
- テーブル, 仕事, 建物, 上, 下
- そこ / それ and この / あの + noun
- Existence with あります / います
- Location particles に / で
- Time questions with 何時に
- Schedule range ～から～まで
- Date question 予約は何月何日ですか
- Relative locations such as 駅の右 and テーブルの上

## Opening retrieval

The first pass showed substantial rust after the break:

- 九時 was not recalled as a whole expression.
- 二十日 was read as はつにち rather than はつか.
- 三人 was used to count waters instead of 三つ.
- 買います was used after 昨日 instead of 買いました.
- テーブル and 仕事 were not recalled.
- この was used as a standalone demonstrative where それ was required.
- 何人 was present in an existence question, but ですか was used instead of いますか.

The core に / で distinction was still partly available even in the weak opening round.

## Repair and retrieval

Repeated retrieval produced strong recovery:

- 九時 → くじ
- 二十日 → はつか
- 仕事 → しごと
- 建物 → たてもの
- 下 → した
- 明日、コンビニで水を三つ買います。
- 駅の右にコンビニがあります。
- ホテルに友達が三人います。
- 昨日、店で水を二つ買いました。
- あの建物はホテルです。
- 昨日、レストランで食べました。

The learner also accurately explained that に marks where someone/something exists, whereas で marks where an action takes place.

## Final mixed review

Initially weak older schedule/question patterns were repaired successfully:

- 明日、何時に行きますか。
- レストランは七時から十時までです。
- 予約は何月何日ですか。
- ホテルに友達が何人いますか。
- テーブルの上に水があります。

Two items remained imperfect at the end:

1. 何人 was finally read as **なんじん** rather than **なんにん**.
2. そこに何ありますか correctly selected そこ and あります, but omitted **が**; target form: そこに何がありますか。

## Outcome

This was productive reactivation rather than new learning. Much of the earlier grammar returned quickly once prompted, so restarting the curriculum is not warranted. The main short-term need is retention of irregular readings and exact sentence frames after the study gap.

Lesson 006 — Frequency and Weekdays remains next. It should begin with a brief retrieval check of 九時, 二十日, 何人, そこに何がありますか, and one mixed time/schedule sentence. Introduce new material only if that short review is stable.
