## New grammar

### Calendar months — number + 月（がつ）

When 月 means a calendar month after a number, read it as **がつ**.

Examples:

- 三月（さんがつ） — March
- 七月（しちがつ） — July
- 十一月（じゅういちがつ） — November

All three were read and understood correctly on first retrieval.

### Calendar dates — number + 日

Many dates above ten use **number + にち**, but some dates have special readings.

Useful forms from this lesson:

- 十三日（じゅうさんにち） — 13th
- 十六日（じゅうろくにち） — 16th
- 十七日（じゅうしちにち） — 17th
- 二十日（はつか） — 20th
- 三十日（さんじゅうにち） — 30th

The learner initially misread 十六日 and twice added にち after はつか before retrieving both correctly.

### Date + に

A specific calendar date can take **に** when it marks when an action occurs:

- 十一月二十日に京都に行きます。 — I will go to Kyoto on November 20th.

This can naturally produce two に particles with different roles:

- 十一月二十日**に** — when
- 京都**に** — destination

When the date itself is the predicate, do not use this action-time に:

- 予約は十一月二十日です。 — The reservation is November 20th.

Contrast:

- 予約は十一月二十日です。
- 十一月二十日に京都に行きます。

The learner initially produced 十一月二十日に予約はです before the contrast was explained.

### 何月何日（なんがつなんにち）

Use **何月何日** to ask the calendar date.

- 予約は何月何日ですか。 — What date is the reservation?

## People counter — ～人

The normal counter for people is 人（にん）, but one and two are irregular.

- 一人（ひとり） — one person
- 二人（ふたり） — two people
- 三人（さんにん） — three people
- 何人（なんにん） — how many people

Useful patterns:

- ホテルに友達が三人います。 — There are three friends at the hotel.
- ホテルに友達が何人いますか。 — How many friends are at the hotel?

The readings were recalled correctly. The learner initially omitted 何人 when asked to form the question, then produced the full sentence correctly on retry.

## General-purpose counter — ～つ

Use ～つ for many ordinary countable things when no more specific counter is needed.

- 一つ（ひとつ） — one thing
- 二つ（ふたつ） — two things
- 三つ（みっつ） — three things

Useful examples:

- 水を二つください。 — Two waters, please.
- 十一月二十日に水を三つ買います。 — I will buy three waters on November 20th.

Both patterns were produced correctly.

## Mixed production and comprehension

Successful examples included:

- 十一月十七日に広島に行きます。
- ホテルに友達が三人います。
- ホテルに友達が何人いますか。
- 水を二つください。
- 十一月二十日に水を三つ買います。
- レストランに友達が二人います。

Harder mixed prompts exposed older weaknesses:

- 十一月二十日にホテルで何人いますか used action-location で with います; corrected to ホテルに.
- 十一月十六日に店に二つ買います used に for the location of buying; corrected to 店で.
- 十一月二十日に友達が二人レストランにいます was interpreted as friends going to the restaurant, exposing an います / 行きます comprehension lapse.
- 三人 + destination + 行きます sentences showed some word-order / unnecessary が instability.

## Session outcome

- **Dates:** progressing well. Month readings were immediately secure; date readings needed repair, especially 十六日 and 二十日, then improved.
- **People counter:** readings and basic existence/question patterns were strong; harder mixed word order remains in learning.
- **～つ counter:** first three forms and practical shopping production were successful.
- **Main weakness:** the older distinction between destination/existence に and action-location で reappeared under heavier mixed production.
- **Secondary weakness:** one comprehension lapse between います and 行きます.
- **Review result:** partial rather than full mastery; new date/counter material can progress, but に / で and います / 行きます remain short-interval repair items.
